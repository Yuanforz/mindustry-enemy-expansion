# 防御 / Defence
#
# 正面 +x 朝向玩家。摆法照着官方地图里敌方基地的做法：
#   · 炮塔成组，一条补给线从背后经过，每座炮塔旁放一个普通路由器直接喂（Marsh、Basin）；
#   · 墙是一条线，不是铺满：挡在朝玩家的一侧，两头包住侧翼，背后留空给补给（Onset、Basin）。
# 前面带 ? 的方块是可有可无的：有地方才放，挡住自己行军路线或者压到岩壁的那几块就不放。
# 同一种炮塔有多种摆法时，写在前面的优先；前面的放不下就用后面的。
#
# The front, +x, faces the player. Laid out the way the bases on the game's own maps are:
# turrets in groups with one supply line passing behind them and a plain router beside each;
# walls as a line on the player's side that wraps round the flanks, open at the back.
# A part written with a ? goes up only where there is room for it.
# Where there are several layouts of one turret, the first that fits and can be supplied is used.

# boost water：以后有了富余的水，就在两座炮之间那一格放一个液体路由器，同时给两座炮通水（射速 250%）。
# boost water: once there is water to spare, a liquid router goes on the tile between the two turrets and
# cools both (two and a half times the rate of fire).
